How ONM works

The number of scenes is a count, not a guess.

k-means needs k up front. ONM asks a different question: how many distinct frames repeat at least CC times? Those frames become the centroids, and their count is the number of clusters.

  1. 1

    Turn frames into vectors

    Sample at 5 fps, convert to greyscale, even out contrast with CLAHE, then measure 379 luminance, texture and edge features and compress them with PCA.

  2. 2

    Count recurring frames

    SDCO scores each frame by how many others sit within threshold T of it. Frames that repeat at least CC times, and are not near an accepted centroid, become centroids.

  3. 3

    Assign and smooth

    Every frame joins its nearest centroid. Short runs are merged and weak boundaries dropped, so a scene cannot flicker on and off.

  4. 4

    Validate and map back

    ECVM reports A(C), the share of frames close to their centroid, and D(C), the share far from it. Labels are mapped back to the original frame rate and each cut snapped to the true frame.

A(C) measures within-cluster association at the reported SL, not scene-detection accuracy. This pipeline targets hard cuts. Slow fades and shots that differ only in colour may be missed.
